Privacy policy and terms

Privacy policy

The short version

The map works without an account, and then everything you record stays on your phone. If you sign in, only the things you choose to share go online. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.

Without signing in

  • Stays on your phone: your rides, waypoints, photos, trail reports, planned trips and settings. They're stored by your browser or the app, not on our servers. Deleting the app (or the site's data) deletes them; the in-app backup file is yours to keep.
  • Your location is used on your phone to show where you are, record rides and plan routes. It isn't sent to us.
  • Map and weather data: map pictures come from the U.S. Geological Survey, and forecasts from the National Weather Service. To get a forecast, the app sends the National Weather Service the locations of your trip's stops. Trail data comes from the Michigan DNR and U.S. Forest Service and is downloaded from our website.
  • Google Maps opens only when you tap a "Drive here" or "Send directions" button. Google's own privacy policy applies there.

If you sign in (Crew & Ride Watch)

Sign-in and online storage are provided by Google Firebase. When you sign in we store:

  • Your account: your email address and the name you choose. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we get the name and email they share.
  • Ride Watch (only while it's turned on and you're recording): your location about once a minute, the path you ride, speed, miles, ride status (riding, stopped, at camp, ended, emergency alert) and your planned route. Anyone who has your link can see this. Only send it to people you trust, and use "Make a new link" to cut off an old one.
  • Crews: your name, which trails you've ridden (not your full ride tracks), trail reports you post (location, type, note and up to 3 small photos) and planned rides you share. Only members of that crew can see them.

Things you didn't share (other rides, waypoints, photos) never leave your phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Online data stays until you remove it. You can stop sharing a ride, make a new family link, remove a report or shared ride, or leave a crew at any time.
  • Delete my account (Layers → Crew & Ride Watch → Delete my account) removes your account and everything of yours stored online. It's immediate and can't be undone. Data on your phone stays until you delete it.

Children

The app isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Questions or a data request: contact the developer through the app store listing.

Terms of use

  • Ride at your own risk. Off-road riding is dangerous. You're responsible for your own safety, your riders and your machine.
  • Always follow posted signs and the law. Trail, closure and road information comes from the Michigan DNR and U.S. Forest Service. It can be out of date or wrong. A closure on the ground counts even if the map doesn't show it.
  • Rules summaries aren't legal advice. The "Rules & permits" page is a plain-English summary with links to the official sources; the official laws are what count.
  • Estimates are guesses. Ride times, routes and Ride Watch's "estimated position" are rough estimates. Never rely on them in an emergency: call 911.
  • Be respectful. Don't post false trail reports or anything offensive in a crew. We may remove content or accounts that misuse the service.
  • The app is provided as-is, without warranties, and we aren't liable for losses from using it, to the extent the law allows.

Michigan ORV Map isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Michigan DNR, the U.S. Forest Service or any trail organization.
