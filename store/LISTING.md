## 4. Full description (Apple and Google, 4000 max)

```
Michigan ORV Map is a free, offline trail map built for Michigan riders: side-by-sides, ATVs and dirt bikes.

EVERY TRAIL, FILTERED TO YOUR MACHINE
• Every Michigan DNR ORV route, ATV trail and motorcycle trail, plus state forest roads and national forest roads open to ORVs.
• Pick your machine (dirt bike, ATV, or side-by-side by width) and the map hides anything you can't legally ride.
• Temporary closures and reroutes, checked against the DNR every night. The app tells you what changed.

WORKS WITH NO SIGNAL
• Save maps for offline, then ride for days with no service. The map, trails, routes and ride recording all work offline.

PLAN THE RIDE
• Route on trails between any spots, with gas stations and campgrounds along the way, overnight stops and multi-day trips.
• "Find me a loop": pick a start and a distance and get loops that favor trail you haven't ridden yet.
• Select trails to add up miles and ride time, or pick just the part of a trail system you'll ride.
• Trail conditions: recent rain along your route, mud warnings and trail reports from your crew.
• County roads open to ORVs, the free camping rules, public hunting land and the deer-season riding hours.

RECORD EVERY RIDE
• Big glove-friendly buttons: hold to pause, mark a spot, camp for the night, SOS.
• Live speed, miles, moving time and distance back to the truck, and one tap to route back to it.
• Photos at the start, end and along the way. Trail reports for mud, washouts and downed trees.
• Ride details with elevation and speed charts, a shareable ride card, and season stats and milestones.

RIDE EVERY TRAIL IN MICHIGAN
• Every trail you ride is colored in. Track your progress toward riding every ORV trail in the state, trail system by trail system.

FAMILY AND CREW (optional sign-in)
• Ride Watch: send someone at home a link. They see where you are on a map. In a dead zone it shows where you probably are, based on your route and speed, until real updates come through again. SOS alerts reach them too.
• Crews: combine progress with your riding group, share trail reports with photos and planned rides, and see the season leaderboard.

SAFETY AND RULES
• An SOS card with your exact location, text-to-911, and the nearest hospital.
• Tell someone your plan, with a reminder to check in.
• Sunset warnings, plus a plain-English guide to Michigan ORV law and permits with links to the official sources.

Free, no ads, no tracking. Your rides stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

Trail data: Michigan DNR and U.S. Forest Service. Not affiliated with the Michigan DNR. Always follow posted signs and the law; ride at your own risk.
```

## 6. Age rating questionnaires

- **Apple:** answer "None" to every content question (violence, profanity, gambling, etc.). For **"user-generated content"**, answer **Yes**: crew members can post names, trail reports, notes and photos inside private crews. Expected result: **4+**. Apple guideline 1.2 asks apps with user content to let people **report** objectionable content and **remove or block** a user. See "Before you submit" below.
- **Google (IARC):** category "Reference, News, or Educational" or "All other app types". Answer "No" to everything except **"Users can interact or exchange content: Yes"**, and **"Shares user's location with other users: Yes"** (Ride Watch). Expected result: **Everyone**.

## 7. Privacy answers

These have to match the privacy policy.

### Apple "App Privacy" (nutrition label)
- **Data used to track you:** none.
- **Data linked to you** (only when signed in):
  - **Contact Info → Email address**: App functionality (sign-in).
  - **Contact Info → Name**: App functionality (shown to your crew and family).
  - **Location → Precise location**: App functionality (Ride Watch, while sharing a ride).
  - **User Content → Photos**: App functionality (photos on shared crew trail reports).
  - **User Content → Other user content**: App functionality (trail reports, planned rides, which trails you've ridden).
  - **Identifiers → User ID**: App functionality (your account).
- **Data not collected:** everything else. Rides, waypoints and photos you don't share never leave the phone.

### Google "Data safety"
- **Collects data:** Yes. **Shares data:** No (sharing with your own crew or family link at your direction isn't "sharing" under Google's definition). **Encrypted in transit:** Yes. **Users can request deletion:** Yes (Delete my account in the app).
- **Types:** Location (precise, optional), Personal info (name, email; optional, for sign-in), Photos (optional), App activity → other user-generated content (optional).
- **Purpose for every type:** App functionality. **Optional:** Yes (the map works without signing in).
